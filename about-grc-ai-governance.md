# About Me — GRC, IT Risk & AI Governance

I am building my cybersecurity career around **GRC, IT risk, AI governance and technical security assurance**. I like work where the question is not only “is there a technical weakness?” but also “what does it mean for the business, which control actually reduces the risk, who owns the decision, and what evidence would an auditor or manager expect?”

My portfolio reflects that approach. I have completed projects covering third-party cloud vendor risk, IAM Joiner–Mover–Leaver governance, AI security governance and incident readiness, risk acceptance and security exception management, and an enterprise Active Directory/Splunk lab.

I also keep a technical foundation in **VAPT, IAM and security operations** so that my GRC work is grounded in how controls behave in practice. That includes hands-on work with Burp Suite, Wireshark, VirusTotal, Splunk, Windows event logs, Active Directory and common security-testing tools.

## What I am strongest at

- Translating technical findings into business risk
- Risk and control assessment
- Security exception and risk-acceptance decisions
- Third-party and cloud vendor risk
- IAM governance, RBAC, least privilege and access reviews
- AI governance, AI risk and human-oversight assessment
- Control evidence, remediation tracking and executive summaries
- NIST CSF 2.0-aligned governance thinking
- Technical security assurance using practical lab evidence
