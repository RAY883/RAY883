01 / Perspective

Governance is where technology becomes accountable.

I am Raymond Amoako, a governance and cybersecurity professional based in Accra, Ghana. My work combines hands-on security assurance with the documentation, risk thinking and stakeholder coordination needed to move technical findings into clear decisions.

I have built practical work around ISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF and Zero Trust principles, with current study focused on the CISA Zero Trust Maturity Model and Federal cybersecurity governance.

The goal is not to produce more documentation. It is to make sure the right decision can be understood, challenged, approved and traced back to the requirement behind it.
